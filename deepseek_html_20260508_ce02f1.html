Grid + Plus Signs

This is the extracted grid system with floating plus signs. The plus signs appear randomly across the screen and fade in/out in organic patches.

✨ Features:
  • 60px and 20px grid backgrounds
  • Red "+" signs floating at ~60% density
  • Signs fade in/out randomly in patches
  • Patches vary in size, shape, and frequency
  • Handles window resize and scroll
