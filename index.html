About

Adham Sinan (b. 1995) is an architect, artist, researcher and educator based in Vienna and Palestine. He is also a PhD candidate at the University of Applied Arts Vienna.

Sinan has participated in and had his work published in international exhibitions and festivals, such as the Venice Biennale 2023, the Chicago Biennale 2023 and the Rotterdam Architecture Biennale 2024, amongst others. His work explores residual matters to craft new spatial and visual possibilities and to redefine redundancy via crafting critical lenses of modernity and what it represents.

Trained as an architect, Sinan's work extends beyond traditional architectural boundaries, engaging with various artistic media and toolsets. He combines his architectural expertise with writing, film, music, and photography, investigating the multifaceted nature of space. His works challenge and expand conventional ideas of possible space through experimentation, shifting perspectives and using defamiliarisation to emphasise spatial identity, interaction, and emotional response.
Practice An architect, artist, and researcher blending conceptual, theoretical, technical, and artistic approaches. Work is enriched by interdisciplinary collaboration and strong research foundations.

Current roles include Faculty Member at the Faculty of Architecture, Arab American University (AAUP), Palestine (2025–present), Researcher/Curator at CSRC at AAUP, and freelance architect worldwide (2022–present). Previously architect at LAVA, Berlin (2024–2025).

Education: PhD / DR.TECH — Die Angewandte (2024–present); MArch — Die Angewandte, Magna cum laude (2020–2024); B.Sc Architecture — AABU, Jordan (2013–2020).
