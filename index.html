About

Architecture as a medium for structural resilience and collective memory.

An architect, artist, and researcher blending conceptual, theoretical, technical, and artistic approaches. My work is enriched by interdisciplinary collaboration and strong research foundations, aiming to create architectural visions that cultivate connection and commonality.

I am interested in how post-disaster contexts generate their own material logic — the specific geometry of conflict rubble, the knowledge embedded in craft traditions, the structural potential of the circular waste economy. My work tests these hypotheses at 1:1 scale, in real conditions, with real communities.

BasedVienna, Austria · Bethlehem, Palestine
FocusRegenerative material practice, post-conflict reconstruction, vernacular knowledge systems
EducationPhD / Die Angewandte — MArch / Die Angewandte — BSc / AABU
ExhibitionsClimate Biennale Vienna 2026, IABR11 Rotterdam, Chicago Architecture Biennial CAB5
